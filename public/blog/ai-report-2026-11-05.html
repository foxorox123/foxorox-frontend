3. Why Lam Research Matters to Semiconductor Manufacturing

Lam Research does not primarily manufacture chips. It manufactures equipment used by semiconductor producers to fabricate them.

Its core technologies include:

  • Plasma etching
  • Thin-film deposition
  • Atomic layer deposition
  • Wafer cleaning
  • Advanced packaging process equipment
  • Installed equipment servicing and upgrades

Modern semiconductor production requires hundreds of processing steps. As transistors become smaller and memory architectures become more complex, the number and precision of deposition and etching processes can increase.

This creates an opportunity for Lam to capture greater equipment spending per advanced wafer.

4. The AI Memory Supercycle

The AI infrastructure expansion has created exceptional demand for memory technologies.

AI accelerators require high-bandwidth memory to transfer data rapidly between memory and processing units.

HBM manufacturing involves advanced DRAM fabrication, wafer thinning, through-silicon vias, bonding and complex packaging.

Lam participates through equipment used in several of these semiconductor manufacturing processes.

The investment opportunity extends beyond HBM.

AI data centers also require large amounts of NAND flash storage for datasets, model checkpoints and high-performance storage systems.

Why this matters: A company can benefit from AI infrastructure growth without selling GPUs or operating AI models. Lam earns revenue when semiconductor manufacturers expand or upgrade fabrication capacity.

5. NAND: A Second Growth Engine

Advanced 3D NAND manufacturing is increasingly complex.

Memory manufacturers build vertically stacked structures containing many layers.

Higher layer counts create demanding high-aspect-ratio etching requirements.

Lam's technological position in etch and deposition makes the company particularly relevant to this manufacturing trend.

A recovery in NAND capital spending could support equipment demand independently of the direct HBM investment cycle.

However, NAND remains a cyclical market. Excess capacity or weaker storage demand could delay customer investment.

6. Advanced Packaging and HBM

AI performance increasingly depends on semiconductor packaging rather than transistor scaling alone.

Advanced packaging allows multiple chips and memory stacks to communicate through high-density interconnects.

The manufacturing process involves complex deposition, etch and metallization requirements.

Lam has identified advanced packaging as an important growth opportunity.

Industry research has highlighted management expectations for advanced packaging revenue growth exceeding 50% during calendar 2026.

This growth should not be confused with 50% growth for the entire company.

15. Is the Semiconductor Equipment Market in a Supercycle?

The semiconductor equipment industry is historically cyclical.

Memory manufacturers periodically expand production capacity, creating periods of strong equipment orders followed by weaker investment.

However, several structural trends may support a longer investment cycle:

  • AI accelerator demand
  • HBM capacity expansion
  • Advanced logic manufacturing
  • Greater memory density requirements
  • Government-supported semiconductor manufacturing
  • New advanced packaging technologies

Lam previously raised its 2026 wafer fabrication equipment market outlook to approximately $140 billion.

The investment case is strongest if increasing manufacturing complexity produces higher equipment spending per wafer, not merely higher wafer volumes.

16. The Difference Between Lam and AI Cloud Providers

AI cloud companies must invest heavily in data centers, accelerators, electricity infrastructure and cooling systems.

Their returns depend on selling sufficient computing services over the economic life of those assets.

Lam's economic exposure is different.

It sells manufacturing equipment to chipmakers that are expanding or modernizing production capacity.

Lam therefore participates earlier in the semiconductor capital investment chain.

Foxorox interpretation: Lam can benefit from the construction of AI semiconductor manufacturing capacity without carrying the full long-term operating risk of a hyperscale data center. However, semiconductor equipment orders remain dependent on the investment budgets of chip manufacturers.
